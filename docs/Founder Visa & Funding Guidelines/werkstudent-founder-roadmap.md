## Your Situation in One Paragraph

You finish your thesis in August/September 2026. Your student visa expires December 31 2026. You work part-time as a pizza baker (unlimited contract). You want to build Werkstudent Match and stay in Berlin to run it. This guide tells you exactly what to do and when, in simple steps.

## ⚠️ 3 Rules to Never Break

1. **Do NOT register a UG or GmbH** before your grant is approved — it kills your BSS eligibility permanently
2. **Do NOT let your visa expire** without applying for the next one first — apply before Dec 31
3. **Do NOT keep the pizza job** once BSS funding starts — it violates the full-time rule and is grant fraud

## Phase 1: Now → August 2026
### What you're doing: Build the product and set up your team

**Step 1 — Keep the pizza job**
Your current student visa allows you to work. Keep earning. You need income until BSS funding arrives.

**Step 2 — Build the MVP**
This is your most important job right now. BSS requires a working prototype before you apply. You do NOT need paying users — just something that works and can be demoed.

Target by August:
- Working swipe + match + generate flow
- 10 real users (friends are fine)
- Eval suite showing your AI beats ChatGPT

**Step 3 — Find co-founders (required for BSS)**
BSS wants 2–4 founders. You need at least 1 co-founder who:
- Lives in Berlin (or will move here)
- Is committed full-time
- Has a skill you don't (marketing, business dev, ops)

Your marketing friend and ops person are the right idea. Lock this in with a written founders agreement before writing serious code. Standard: 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff.

**Step 4 — Find a university professor mentor**
This is the part most people miss. BSS requires a professor from a supporting Berlin university to mentor your project. UE Berlin is NOT in the BSS consortium, so you need a mentor from one of these:
- TU Berlin
- FU Berlin
- HU Berlin
- HTW Berlin ← easiest for tech startups
- BHT Berlin
- HWR Berlin

How to find one: email professors whose research touches AI, labor markets, or HR tech. Keep it short — explain the project, ask if they'd be willing to be a BSS mentor. Most professors say yes because it costs them little.

**Step 5 — Start the public build log**
Post on LinkedIn once a week. Show what you're building. This builds credibility before you need it. Investors and grant reviewers Google you.

## Phase 2: August/September 2026
### What you're doing: Thesis done → Get the right visa immediately

**Step 6 — Apply for the §20 Job Seeker Permit the day you graduate**

This is your legal safety net. Every German university graduate (non-EU) is entitled to this. Do it immediately after graduation, before December 31.

What it gives you:
- 18 months to stay in Germany
- Unlimited work rights (you can keep the pizza job AND build the startup)
- Time to get BSS approved without visa pressure

What you need to apply:
- Proof of graduation (degree certificate or confirmation from UE)
- Valid passport
- Berlin registration (Anmeldung)
- Proof of funds (~€1,091/month — blocked account or similar)
- Current residence permit (your student visa)

Where to apply: LEA Berlin (Landesamt für Einwanderung) — book the appointment online at service.berlin.de. Book 8–10 weeks before your visa expires. Berlin is slow.

You'll get a **Fiktionsbescheinigung** (a paper document) while they process your application. This keeps your rights alive. Carry it everywhere with your passport.

**Step 7 — Book an immigration lawyer consultation (once)**
Cost: €150–300. Worth every cent. Ask specifically:
- Does my §20 permit allow me to participate in BSS?
- When should I apply for the §21 founder permit?
- What documents do I need for the permit switch?

## Phase 3: September → November 2026
### What you're doing: Apply for BSS

**Step 8 — Book your BSS consultation**
Go to science-startups.berlin and book a free consultation. They will:
- Check if you're eligible
- Tell you which batch to apply for
- Give you the application link

Also check HTW Berlin and BHT Startup Hub — they run their own BSS lines with the same money and may be easier to access as an external team.

**Step 9 — Prepare your BSS application**

What BSS wants to see:
- Working prototype (you have this from Phase 1)
- Team of 2–4 founders
- Professor mentor confirmed
- Berlin residence for all founders
- Innovative tech concept
- You have NOT started selling yet (no economic activity)

What BSS does NOT require:
- Paying users
- Revenue
- A registered company
- German citizenship

**Step 10 — Confirm visa compatibility in writing**
Before submitting, email the BSS incubator and ask: *"I hold a §20 job-seeker permit. Does this satisfy your residence permit requirement for the funding period?"* Get their answer in writing. Also ask the Ausländerbehörde the same question. This protects you.

## Phase 4: December 2026 → March 2027
### What you're doing: BSS decision arrives, visa switches

**What happens:**
- Your student visa expires Dec 31 — but you're safe because you have the §20 permit (or Fiktionsbescheinigung)
- BSS jury pitch happens (roughly 4–6 weeks after application closes)
- BSS decision arrives

**Step 11 — When BSS is approved: switch to §21 founder permit**

Use the BSS award letter to apply for a **§21(2b)** or **§21(2a)** residence permit. This is the official "founder/self-employment" permit.

Your immigration lawyer handles this. It takes up to 3 months to process. You stay on §20 + Fiktionsbescheinigung during processing.

**Step 12 — End the pizza job before BSS funding starts**
BSS requires full-time commitment (35 hrs/week). Employment must be terminated before the stipend begins. Do this only once funding is confirmed in writing.

## Phase 5: March 2027 onwards
### What you're doing: Full-time on the startup

**Monthly income during BSS:**
- 1 founder: €2,500/month
- 2 founders: €5,000/month total
- 3 founders: €7,500/month total

This is your runway. Use it to build, find users, and get to €2,000+ MRR before BSS ends.

**Step 13 — Register the company (UG) during BSS**
Once BSS is confirmed, register a UG (haftungsbeschränkt). Cost: ~€300–500 via a notary. You need this to open a business bank account and eventually charge customers.

Note: BSS requires you have NOT started economic activity before funding starts — so register the company during the funding period, not before.

**Step 14 — Launch paid tier and track MRR**
Around month 3–4 of BSS, turn on payments. €5.99/month. Even 50 paying users is proof the model works.

**Decision point at month 5 of BSS:**
- Growing? → Stay the course, apply for next funding stage
- Flat? → Pivot to employer-side B2B, or wind down gracefully

## Phase 6: After BSS (Month 12–18)
### What you're doing: Scale or raise

**Option A — GründungsBONUS Plus (IBB)**
Up to €50,000 non-repayable grant from the State of Berlin. Requirements:
- Company registered (UG/GmbH)
- Less than 18 months old
- Tech/digital business model
- Berlin headquarters

Apply at ibb.de. No nationality restriction. This is your bridge between BSS ending and angel money.

**Option B — Angel investors**
Target once you have €2,000–5,000 MRR. Typical round: €150,000–500,000 for 10–20% equity.

Best places to find angels in Berlin:
- High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF) — Germany's most active early-stage fund
- APX (Axel Springer + Porsche) — invests €50k for 5%, very early stage
- Berlin Founders Hub — meetups and intros
- Entrepreneur First — helps find co-founders AND funds you

**Option C — Stay profitable, don't raise**
If you reach €5,000–10,000 MRR with 2 founders, that's €2,500–5,000/month each. A good Berlin salary with 100% ownership. Not every startup needs VC money.

## Things to Ask at Your BSS Consultation

Copy these questions and bring them:

1. Our founder studied at UE Berlin (not a consortium university) — can we apply as an external team?
2. Which supporting university would you recommend we find a mentor at?
3. I will hold a §20 job-seeker permit during the funding period — does that satisfy your residence requirement?
4. What batch should we target given our August/September 2026 graduation?
5. Our company will not be incorporated before funding starts — is that correct per your rules?

## Things to Ask at Your Immigration Lawyer Appointment

1. I have a §20 permit — can I legally participate in BSS and do startup work?
2. When should I apply for the §21(2b) or §21(2a) permit?
