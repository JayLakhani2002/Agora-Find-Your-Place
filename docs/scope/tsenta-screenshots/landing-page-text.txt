How does Tsenta find jobs?
–

Tsenta watches 50,000+ company career pages directly. The moment a new role goes live that fits your résumé and preferences (location, salary, experience level, role family), it lands in your match feed within seconds, with a clear breakdown of why it matched. You also get a curated daily list, and you can paste any job URL to add it to your queue.

How do I know if Tsenta applied correctly?
+

Every submitted application gets a receipt: the exact fields that were filled, the answers given to open-ended questions, the résumé and cover letter that went out, and a confirmation back from the ATS. You can review every application after the fact and flag anything you'd want changed for next time.

Will recruiters know I used Tsenta?
+

No. Applications go through the same standard forms a manual applicant would. The résumé and cover letter are tailored to the specific role using your real background, and open-ended answers are written in your voice from context you've provided. There's no automated flag in the submission.

How does résumé tailoring work?
+

For each role, Tsenta reads the job description, identifies the keywords and skills the recruiter is screening for, and rewrites your résumé to align them — using only true facts from the résumé you uploaded. You can save the tailored versions per company, so a follow-up or second application doesn't redo the work.

I'm on OPT or need sponsorship — does Tsenta help?
+

Yes. Set your work authorization status once (OPT, STEM-OPT, H-1B, citizen, etc.) and Tsenta filters out roles where the company doesn't sponsor, and answers the work-authorization questions on every application correctly the first time. We surface the sponsorship signals we know about per company, but we don't make guarantees on the company's behalf.

Is there a free plan?
+

Yes — your first 25 applications are free, no card required. You get the full product on the free tier; upgrade only when you want to keep going.
