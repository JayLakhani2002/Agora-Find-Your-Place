# Agora — Strategic Plan
**Document:** STRAT-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** June 2026 · **Owner:** Founding Team

> Horizon framing: **Strategy Year 1 = the build-and-prove year (Jul 2026 → Jun 2027).** Because BSS rules prohibit economic activity before the grant is funded (~Mar 2027), *monetization* Year 1 begins ~Q2 2027. This plan uses **build-calendar quarters** for Year-1 goals and notes the monetization clock where it matters.

---

## 1. Three-Year Vision

**Mission:** Help every young person in Europe *find their place* — the work, and the footing, in a new system.

**Vision (June 2029):** Agora is the default way international students and young job seekers in the DACH region find legal, well-matched work — a two-sided marketplace where job seekers get eligibility-checked matches and ATS-ready applications, and employers get a verified, pre-scored candidate pool. The legal-depth + ATS-quality moat, built first in Germany, is being rolled out by configuration across the EU.

| Year | Theme | End-state | Primary proof point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Year 1** (Jul 26–Jun 27) | **Prove the wedge** | Working product, validated quality, BSS funded, billing live, first paying users in Berlin | ATS pass rate ≥90%; ≥3.5% conversion signal; first MRR |
| **Year 2** (Jul 27–Jun 28) | **Own Berlin, prove repeatability** | Native app, Hamburg live, ~€10k MRR exit rate, B2B recruiter pilots | ~€9k+ MRR; first paying recruiters; LTV:CAC ≥5:1 |
| **Year 3** (Jul 28–Jun 29) | **Scale two-sided + expand** | Munich + DACH config, recruiter SaaS at scale, Series-A-ready metrics | ~€40k+ MRR exit; B2B = meaningful revenue line; expansion playbook documented |

**Strategic pillars (constant across all 3 years):**
1. **Depth before breadth** — win one square completely before expanding.
2. **Quality over volume** — the human-approval gate and eval suite are the brand.
3. **Community is the channel** — near-zero CAC until unit economics are proven.
4. **Capital-efficient by design** — non-dilutive grants cover survival; equity buys growth.

## 5. Strategic Decision Gates

| Gate | Timing | Trigger → Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Quality gate** | Dec 2026 | ATS <90% or eval <8.0 → hold launch, fix generation before scaling |
| **Funding gate** | Feb/Mar 2027 | BSS denied → activate GründungsBONUS Plus / angel fallback; lean to free-tier growth |
| **Monetization gate** | BSS month 5 (~Jul 2027) | Conversion <1% & no MRR growth → test €2.99 tier; accelerate B2B pivot |
| **Expansion gate** | Mid 2028 | Berlin unit economics proven (LTV:CAC ≥5:1) → fund Hamburg + native app; else deepen Berlin |
| **Scale gate** | Mid 2029 | B2B traction + €40k MRR → raise Series A; else extend runway and optimize |
