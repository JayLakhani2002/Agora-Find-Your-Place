## The one-paragraph version

Agora is a mobile-first, swipe-to-apply job platform for job seekers aged 18–35 in Europe. We start with the hardest, highest-pain wedge — **international students in Berlin** navigating Germany's labyrinthine work-eligibility rules — and we are the only platform that models those rules (140-day rule, 20-hour cap, Minijob ceiling, Chancenkarte §20a) as **hard filters**, generates German-ATS-tested CVs and cover letters per application, and never auto-submits without the user's click. Our AI guide, **Ari**, walks beside the user the whole way. We make money through a B2C freemium subscription today and a B2B recruiter subscription from month 18. The wedge is defensible (6–12 months of German legal/ATS depth no US tool has), the founder is the target user, and the first 12+ months of founder runway are covered by a non-dilutive Berlin grant (BSS) — so a pre-seed euro buys growth, not survival.
