# Agora — Investor Pitch Summary (One-Pager)
**Pre-seed · Berlin · June 2026 · *Find your place.***

---

## The one-liner
**Agora is the swipe-to-apply job platform for 18–35s in Europe** — starting where the pain is sharpest: international students in Berlin who can't tell which jobs they're even *legally allowed* to take. We're the only platform that models Germany's work-eligibility rules as hard filters, generates German-ATS-tested CVs and cover letters per application, and lets the user click submit — guided the whole way by **Ari**, our AI companion character.

---

## The problem
Europe's most motivated job seekers are served worst. An international student in Germany faces three walls at once: **legal complexity** (20-hour cap, 140-day rule, €556 Minijob ceiling, Chancenkarte §20a — modeled by *no* major platform), **document barriers** (German *Tabellarischer Lebenslauf* + ATS systems that reject generic ChatGPT output), and **brutal friction** (hours per application, high abandonment). Spray-and-pray auto-appliers made it worse — recruiter blacklisting and <2% callback rates are now the norm in that category.

## The solution
**Match → generate → track, in under 5 minutes.** Swipe a deck of *only legally-eligible* jobs → right-swipe → AI drafts a tailored CV + cover letter → 6-dimension quality/ATS check (auto-regenerate if it fails) → you review and submit → tracked pipeline with auto-drafted follow-ups → interview prep. **Never auto-submitted.** Ari guides every step.

---

## Why now
- **The wedge is open and time-boxed.** Germany is largely untapped — only one shallow local rival (CareerBoom.ai); US tools (Sorce, AIApply) need **6–12 months** of German legal/ATS work to compete here. The window is *now*.
- **AI just made the hard part cheap.** Per-application tailored, ATS-passing document generation costs ~€0.06–0.10 today — impossible to do at quality and price two years ago.
- **Regulatory tailwind.** GDPR + EU AI scrutiny *punish* US-built, US-hosted, mass-apply tools and *reward* an EU-only, GDPR-first, human-in-the-loop platform. We're built the way the regulation wants.
- **Seasonal trigger.** The October Wintersemester arrival wave is a recurring, predictable acquisition spike we're built to capture.

## Why us
- **The founder is the user.** A Berlin international student living this exact pain, with *direct access to the WhatsApp/Telegram/campus communities that are the distribution channel* — not replicable by a US/UK team in Year 1.
- **Three complementary co-founders:** CEO (tech/product), COO (ops/legal/compliance), CMO (community-led growth).
- **Judgment on display.** A documented eval suite, a written "do-not-build" list (Mode 3 auto-submit permanently excluded on legal grounds), and a real financial model — discipline, not just enthusiasm.
